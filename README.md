# 🤖 Xilum TF - Combat Robot CAD Models

Welcome to the mechanical design repository for **Xilum TF**, a custom-built combat robot designed for Robowars and robotics competitions. This repository contains the complete mechanical CAD files, including the full 3D assembly and individual parts ready for manufacturing.

## 🚀 About the Mechanical Design
Xilum TF is engineered to withstand heavy impacts in the arena while keeping the weight balanced for agile movement and high-torque driving. 

### Design Highlights:
* **Chassis & Armor:** Custom-designed base plates and armor optimized for impact resistance and weight distribution.
* **Drive System Integration:** Precision-measured mounts for heavy-duty stepper motors.
* **Steering & Weapon Mounts:** Secure mounting points for high-torque servos and arena combat mechanisms.

## 📂 CAD Files Structure
The designs are organized for easy viewing, 3D printing, and laser cutting:

* `/Assembly` - Contains the full master assembly of the robot (`.step` format).
* `/3D_Printed_Parts` - Individual `.stl` files for 3D printing (e.g., motor mounts, brackets, internal structures).
* `/Laser_Cut_Profiles` - Flat `.dxf` files for CNC routing or laser cutting armor and base plates from metal or polycarbonate.

*(Note: You can open the `.step` files in any major CAD software like Fusion 360, SolidWorks, or AutoCAD to view the complete robot).*
